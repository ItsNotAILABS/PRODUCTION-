## PREAMBLE

These MEGA LAWS constitute the complete legal and operational framework governing the Sovereign Organism. They are organized into ten domains, each with binding rules that all protocols, engines, agents, and bots must obey.

**Enforcement**: All laws are evaluated by the governance engine. Violations trigger automatic actions: FORBID, REQUIRE, ALLOW, ESCALATE, or NOTIFY.
